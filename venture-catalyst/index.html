Venture Catalyst

Months and months of work in eight weeks.

An eight-week accelerator for a small group. You pick one clear goal and work towards it every week. If you are up for some rigorous work, we would like you to be a part of it.

Starts the first week of every month

How it works

  1. Attend a Chemistry Labs experience or get recommended by a friend.
  2. We talk to you and understand your goal.
  3. Selected founders meet and work towards their goal every week for eight weeks.

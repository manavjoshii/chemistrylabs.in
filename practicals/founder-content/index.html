My reels get no views. Will this fix that? +

Views come from the first three seconds and from having something worth saying. Both get worked on here. The algorithm does not, and nobody in the room will pretend otherwise.

I hate being on camera. +

Most founders do. You still hit record, because that is the only thing that makes it smaller. You choose the format and faceless counts. You leave with a piece either way.

I post for two weeks and then stop. +

That happens to almost everyone, and it is a planning problem. You leave with a plan you wrote yourself, sized to the hours you actually have.

Can't I just pay someone to make my content? +

You can, and plenty of people will take the money. They can shoot and they can cut. They cannot have your point of view, and that is the part that makes a founder's content work. Learn it once, then hand over the rest.

What do I bring? +

A charged phone with free storage. No professional gear is expected. If you own a tripod or a mic, bring them. Don't write a script beforehand.

How long, and where? +

9 AM, not 9:15. It ends when your piece is made, which is about three hours. A centrally located venue in Ahmedabad, and you get the address with your invite.

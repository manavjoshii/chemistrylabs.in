My project isn't that ambitious. Should I still apply? +

The bar is that you're actually building it. Not how big it looks.

I'm not building an app. Does this count? +

Builders are not just coders. Writers, filmmakers, founders, students. If you're making something, this is for you.

What do I bring? +

An empty mind and your favourite diary. We'll figure the rest.

How long, and where? +

10 AM to 2 PM. The city changes from one Sunday to the next, so every date in the form says where that one is. You get the address with your invite.

I applied. Then what? +

We read every one. A few get an invite that week. If it isn't you, your request doesn't die, it carries to the next Sunday and sits above the new ones. You never have to apply twice.
